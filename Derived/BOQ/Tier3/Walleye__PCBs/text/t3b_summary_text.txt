Generalized Additive Models (GAMs) were used to model the relationship between fish length and pcbs concentrations for walleye in the AOC and reference regions. The length-contaminant relationship did not differ significantly between sites (interaction test p = 0.215). Marginal mean concentrations were estimated across the full size distribution using a reduced model with shared smooths. The estimated marginal mean PCBs concentration in the AOC was 3.3 units (95% CI: 2.95–3.64). Estimated values for reference site(s): Reference: 3.64 units (95% CI: 3.35–3.93).
