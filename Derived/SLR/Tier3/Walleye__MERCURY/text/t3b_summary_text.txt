Generalized Additive Models (GAMs) were used to model the relationship between fish length and Mercury concentrations for Walleye in the AOC and reference site(s) (Reference). The length–contaminant relationship differed significantly between sites (interaction test p = 0.0493). Model-predicted concentrations were calculated across the observed size range for which observations existed for both regions (every 5 cm from 20 to 70 cm) and are presented in Table \@ref(tab:tier-3b-summary-table). Asterisks (*) denote sizes where the AOC differs significantly (p < 0.05) from one or more reference site(s); this occurred at 11 of 11 lengths evaluated.
